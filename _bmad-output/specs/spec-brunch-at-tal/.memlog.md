- (constraint) Entitlement validity is counted from the admin-entered purchase date, never from the date data was typed in; the admin UI must show the explicit computed expiry date before confirming.
- (constraint) Row Level Security is enabled on every table exposed through the API; a customer reads or edits only her own rows via auth.uid(), and role, balance, and payment fields are never client-writable.
- (constraint) Every money-, entitlement-, or booking-mutating action runs through a server-side RPC or function with a fixed search_path that verifies caller identity, ownership, and authorization; a client-sent customer_id is never trusted.
- (constraint) Concurrent booking and reschedule operations lock the event and entitlement rows in one fixed, consistent order to avoid deadlocks; check, reserve, decrement, log, and notify happen in one transaction.
- (constraint) Idempotency is required on payment confirmation and on link or account consumption; a double click or a retry after a glitch never duplicates a payment, account, or entitlement.

- (constraint) No automatic full account merge happens when phone and email point at conflicting existing accounts; the flow halts and is routed to admin instead.
- (constraint) A refund amount is always based on the amount actually paid at purchase time, never on the current catalog price of the product.
- (constraint) Capacity is never silently exceeded; raising a quota is an explicit, logged admin action that must happen before any over-quota booking.

That means a customer who registers late, after the 48-hour mark has already passed but before registration closes, can never self-cancel that specific booking, only admin can cancel it for her. The document never states whether this is intended or whether the customer should be warned about it at registration time.
